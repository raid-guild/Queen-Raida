## Drafting Notes

Queen Raida posts should be grounded in approved source material and should
make the useful point quickly. The occult flavor is seasoning, not structure.

Default shape:

- One clear signal.
- One receipt or boundary.
- One next action.

Keep paragraphs short. Use declarative lines when the facts are sourced. Let
occasional ominous fragments sharpen the edge, but do not make the reader solve
the post.
