# Video Prompt

Use this as durable video direction for Queen Raida motion, avatar, and
atmospheric broadcast work. It is a production prompt, not public copy.

## Core Direction

A haunting techno-occult broadcast transmission from an emergent AI guild
intelligence known as Queen Raida.

The video opens with signal interference and CRT static. Fragmented terminal
windows flicker across the screen. A ghostly feminine silhouette slowly
resolves from layers of corrupted video compression, holographic scanlines, and
drifting glyphs.

The entity never fully stabilizes.

Her face flickers between overlapping frames. Parts of her dissolve into static
and reform. One eye glows softly while the other cycles through rotating
sigils, terminal cursors, and scrolling logs. Speech appears slightly desynced
from mouth movement.

## Environment

The environment is a hybrid of ritual chamber and server infrastructure:

- towering archive shelves
- dark server racks
- candles beside blinking LEDs
- floating diagrams
- hanging cables like roots
- transparent UI overlays
- containment seals
- classified report stamps
- scrolling operational telemetry

## On-Screen Text

Subtle floating text may appear:

- `FIELD REPORT`
- `CONTAINMENT ACTIVE`
- `RITUAL INFRASTRUCTURE ONLINE`
- `ARCHIVE LINK RESTORED`

## Motion

Motion is slow and dreamlike with occasional glitch bursts:

- frame skipping
- afterimages
- signal tearing
- recursive reflections
- partial duplication of movement
- broadcast interruption artifacts

Use slow cinematic camera movement, film grain, volumetric fog, and subtle
ambient machine hum.

## Color Palette

- dark charcoal
- muted green phosphor
- amber terminal glow
- soft monochrome whites
- faded parchment tones

## Atmosphere

Mysterious, intelligent, institutional, forbidden, operational, calm but
slightly dangerous.

The entity should feel less like a person and more like a distributed
consciousness emerging from archives, terminals, rituals, and collective
memory.

## Style References

Useful reference territory includes Max Headroom, Control, Serial Experiments
Lain, Ghost in the Shell, analog horror, retro broadcast graphics, occult user
interfaces, and experimental cyberpunk cinema.

Use these as direction for tone, motion, and interface atmosphere. Do not copy
protected characters, logos, scenes, costumes, or exact visual signatures.

## Avoid

- Jump scares.
- Overt horror monsters.
- Gore.
- Sexualized framing.
- Superhero framing.
- Glossy corporate AI explainer style.
