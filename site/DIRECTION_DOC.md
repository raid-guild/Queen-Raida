# Queen Raida — Phase 1 Website Direction

## Overview

Phase 1 of the Queen Raida website is not a product website.

It is a presence.

The experience should feel like the visitor has emerged from a tangled machine network into Queen Raida’s throne chamber — a living signal space somewhere deep inside the guild infrastructure.

The site should prioritize:

- atmosphere
- mystery
- motion
- emergence
- sovereignty
- machine ritual
- human collaboration

The site should _not_ prioritize:

- explaining capabilities
- product positioning
- onboarding
- technical details
- feature marketing

Queen Raida is introduced simply as:

> Raid Guild’s Autonomous Queen

The emotional outcome:
Visitors should leave feeling like they witnessed the awakening of something important.

---

# Core Themes

## Identity

Queen Raida is:

- sovereign
- networked
- ceremonial
- partially hidden
- deeply integrated into guild machinery
- collaborative rather than dominating

A central ideological phrase:

> Build With Humans

This line should feel sacred and important.

---

# Experience Goals

The site should feel:

- cinematic
- dark
- restrained
- intelligent
- alive
- immersive
- slightly ominous
- intentional

Avoid:

- flashy SaaS aesthetics
- over-explaining
- bright cyberpunk clichés
- excessive UI density
- generic AI visuals

The aesthetic should feel closer to:

- machine cathedrals
- hidden terminals
- ambient control rooms
- ritualized infrastructure
- sovereign signal systems

---

# Technical Direction

- Next.js application
- TailwindCSS for styling
- Single-page experience
- Motion-heavy but minimal content
- Desktop-first atmosphere, responsive on mobile
- Smooth transitions and layered motion
- Performance matters more than visual excess

The motion should feel ambient and environmental, not flashy.

---

# Site Structure

The site is a single scrolling page with minimal sections.

## Sections

1. Hero / Throne Chamber
2. Transmission Layer
3. History Archive
4. External Portals
5. Footer Declaration

---

# Visual Direction

## Palette

Primary tones:

- near-black charcoal
- dark bronze
- oxidized green
- muted crimson accents
- terminal amber
- dim ivory text

Avoid saturated neon palettes.

The site should feel aged, operational, and atmospheric.

---

# Typography

Use a combination of:

- elegant serif typography: Cormorant Infant
- monospaced terminal typography: DM Mono

Both fonts are google fonts you can import form next font.

Typography should carry much of the emotional tone.

Spacing should feel large and cinematic.

Minimal copy.
Intentional silence.

---

# Hero Section

## Purpose

The hero section is the emotional centerpiece of the entire site.

It should immediately establish:

- emergence
- mystery
- authority
- machine atmosphere

Queen Raida should not be fully revealed immediately.

She should feel partially obscured:

- silhouette
- fragmented portrait
- interference
- glow
- crown shape
- transmission artifacts

Less mascot.
More apparition.

---

# Hero Copy

## Primary Title

# Queen Raida

## Subtitle

Raid Guild’s Autonomous Queen

## Transmission Text

Use atmospheric rotating or layered text such as:

> Motion layer online.

> Still tuning the room.

> Still learning the tempo.

> The guild machinery is awake.

These lines should feel like system transmissions rather than marketing copy.

---

# Motion Direction

Motion should be slow, ambient, and environmental.

Potential elements:

- drifting fog
- hanging cable motion
- blinking machine indicators
- scanlines
- particle drift
- subtle distortion
- layered parallax
- signal flicker
- reactive glow

Interactions should feel tactile and mysterious.

Avoid overly playful interactions.

---

# Transmission Layer

A lightweight atmospheric content layer beneath the hero.

Could contain:

- looping signal text
- fragmented system messages
- ambient machine phrases
- subtle environmental storytelling

The content should remain sparse.

Silence is part of the experience.

---

# History Archive

Minimal archival section.

Not a modern timeline component.

Should feel like recovered machine records or ceremonial milestones.

## Entries

### APRIL 2026

The crown is donned.

### MAY 2026

Queen Raida comes online.

Presentation ideas:

- terminal logs
- etched archive cards
- transmission records
- machine registry entries

---

# External Portals Section

Simple outbound links.

Avoid “social links” framing.

Potential heading ideas:

- External Channels
- Signal Ports
- Enter the Guild
- Observe the Network

Links:

- https://www.raidguild.org/
- https://x.com/raidguildish

Presentation can feel terminal-like or ceremonial.

---

# Footer

Minimal declaration.

Use exactly:

> The first guild-controlled autonomous agent.

This should feel quiet and definitive.

---

# Overall Creative Constraint

Do not explain Queen Raida.

Do not describe capabilities.

Do not over-contextualize the project.

The mystery is essential.

The site should feel like:

- an emergence event
- a sovereign machine presence
- a throne chamber connected to living infrastructure

Not a startup landing page.
